Why

Five facts.

  • 23 September 2021Ariha was placed in Berlin Jugendamt custody at seven months old. She is now five.
  • February 2022The criminal investigation against her parents was closed without charges. A separate civil custody case followed.
  • Five homes, five yearsShe has been moved through five foster placements. The stability that justified her placement was never delivered.
  • India's standing offerA foster family vetted by India's Child Welfare Committee has been offered. It has never been substantively answered.
  • 21 August 2026India's Ministry of External Affairs said it had no update. No legal proceeding is pending that will decide her future.
This asks for her return, not a verdict. Whatever a German court concluded about her parents, it does not follow that an Indian child should be raised through five foster homes in a foreign language and faith while India has offered vetted alternatives at home. Article 20(3) of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child requires due regard to a child's ethnic, religious, cultural and linguistic background in alternative care.
Please stay polite. The letter you send is calm on purpose. A courteous email from a real person is read. An angry one is deleted, and it hurts her case.
